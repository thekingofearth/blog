Time is all we have and don't

30% of 2022 is over already. Yes, you read that right. Even I was startled at first. Time indeed flies in a jiffy. It is so subtle that until you know, it already has flown out of your window. Since few days I was thinking about this. Taking an account of time passed and progress on plans made & it is utmost necessary. Doing this you find that many a times plans need to be replanned. Replanning causes guilt of not performing up to how your planned self should have. Now, you want to achieve all things you planned for the whole year in the remaining time. So, if you made only 10% progress in work to be accomplished in an year, you would now wish to transform yourself next morning itself and will plan and be set out to achieve to remaining 90% work in 70% of time and the cycle repeats again & again. If you have been/ are here, now & then...well... you're not alone because majority of people are here everyday. So, the real question is what should we do about it? I don't have conclusive answer but I am working on some hacks that make me achieve my goals. I share with you today one which I am trying out. The hack is over-commitment of performance. Over-commit on goals to be achieved and even advertise it to friends and family around. Don't plan too rationally. Creating artificial external pressure may stimulate better results. Things that are uncontrollable may not ne sped up but controllables can. E.g. if you can learn a skill rationally in 3 months, overcommit to acheive it in say 2 months. Then try your best to so. Avoid replanning to least possible then. You may not achieve what you were set out for but you would be suprised on how much can be accomplished. I have got this...you can say... "hack" from the likes of the irrational time-lines of Elon Musk. So, tell me what you do think....
