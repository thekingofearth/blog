On Knowing Nothing

A person who knows little often make the folly to over estimate his knowledge. Ignorance more frequently begets confidence than does knowledge. Dunning-Kruger effect is the name of this rather paradoxical phenomenon where the sign of how much more you know is rather the knowledge of how much more is yet to know. Keeping this in mind often helps me to tame overconfidence. May be it can help you too. So, you can say in the end "knowing nothing" is the purpose of the quest of life. Also, I know its very inconsistent of me here. May be I am too a prey to this effect. So, working on that part.
