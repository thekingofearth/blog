How to know if you like what you do?

A small one today!

I often pondered upon the thought that - How will I find the work that I like doing the most? I still cannot say I have found the work I love. But the search is still on. However, a man must keep himself busy till he finds his purpose, and lemme tell you, it's a journey. I realised much later that finding your life mojo(s) is a journey and won't be served to you on a platter one fine day. You will discover it bit by bit. Plus, you won't find it if you don't look for it.

And, this is not just about your work/ purpose. The same applies to all the routine things that one does. Say fitness goals, hobby goals, and anything you name that you do to keep yourself busy day in and day out. But predominantly work-related. So, how do you know that you like what you do? I have developed a simple test that solves this. And its fairly SIMPLE if you'll think DEEPLY about it.

And it is - just check if you are willing to do something about it on a WEEKEND. That's it. It must be without any compulsion or pressure. Say you read about some new developments about your job on a weekend. That means you like your job. You try to go to gym and hit your diet on a Saturday too. You like fitness. And so on. It's that simple. Apply this test to your work and life and see if you like what you do. If you don't, then maybe it's time to change it. And if you do, then keep doing it and keep improving yourself.

So, try apply this thought in your work/ life and tell me what you think!

Until next time! Cheers!
