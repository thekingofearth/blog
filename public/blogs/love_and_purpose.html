Love and Purpose

"Love and work....work and love, thats all there is" - Sigmund Freud

Today I am writing a rather off-topic blog. Just something that I had in mind for quite a long time. I don't know of it's being right or wrong for some things are more for believing I guess and only that I am finding some truth in it. Time will test my belief I guess. But first let me tell what i am talking about.
I think that life is two things: Love and Purpose. That's it. Its this simple. Isn't it? Everything one does and everything that happens to him ends unto these only. I think you would too believe it. But this blog is not just to tell you this belief. The real question is...what should a man seek? Love or Purpose? I think above everything in this world; a man must seek his purpose for its his duty to do so. When a man is born so is born his purpose alongwith. One must not disregard his duty for the worldly pleasures. They are important too but not at the cost of one's call of duty.
Talking about love...I don't think one can seek love and find it. Love finds you. The sheer tragedy of a man is that he seeks love and not his purpose. Look, I may not sound logical but this a thought I am coming at again and again I don't know why. I am not saying not to make any efforts to get your love if at all. Just that you cannot deliberately find it. Can you? I would really like to know what you all "believe" for this matter of heart and mind.

I know I am very indisciplined and there is a lot to share. Thoughts are getting collected speedily but I am not putting it out at the same pace. So thinking of doing blog marathons to make well for the lost time as quickly as possible. Trying to get my YouTube channel going too. Will keep you posted. Until next time...Cheers!😉
